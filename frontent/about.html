About us

Indira Gandhi Engineering College Sagar, MP has been a leading name in technical education for over four decades. Our mission is to nurture talent through quality teaching, industry exposure, and hands-on training, preparing students to excel in their professional careers.

We offer undergraduate programs across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Information Technology, and Electronics & Communication Engineering, supported by experienced faculty, modern laboratories, and strong industry connections that help our students secure good placements.
